sleepwalk

Shut your MacBook. Claude keeps working.

A Claude Code mod for macOS. One switch keeps a session running with the lid shut, then turns sleep back on when the work ends.

The screen stays unlocked while it's on. Keep the Mac on a hard surface, never in a bag.

Another way to install

Desktop app: + next to the prompt → Plugins → Add marketplace → adamalshoomary/sleepwalk → Add plugin.

Then turn it on

Click the moon ☾ in the footer under the prompt, beside the model name, or type /lid on. In a new chat the moon shows after your first message. Enter your password, then shut the lid.

Before you shut itUse Auto or Bypass permissions, or approve from your phone with Remote Control.
When it ends2 minutes after the work, after 2 hours, at 25% battery, or if the Mac gets hot.
Nothing left behindNothing stays installed. A shut Mac goes straight back to sleep.
